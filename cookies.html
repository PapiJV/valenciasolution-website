Cookie Policy

Last updated: November 16, 2026

The short version

  • We set one cookie, and only if you claim a free Notepad.
  • We use no advertising or analytics cookies and no tracking pixels.
  • Because the only cookie is essential, there is no cookie banner.

1. The cookie we set

NameWhenWhat it doesHow long
vs_claimWhen you claim the free Notepad offerLets the browser you signed up in get your app link again. It holds a scrambled code, never your email address, and is only sent to our sign-up service.Up to 400 days

You can delete this cookie in your browser settings at any time. If you do, email us from the address you signed up with and we'll resend your app link.

2. Browser storage

When you arrive from a link with a campaign tag, we keep that tag in your browser's session storage so we know which link brought you if you sign up. It is erased when you close the tab.

Each Notepad app saves your team data in your browser's storage on your own device so the app works. See section 3 of our Privacy Policy.

3. Analytics

We use Vercel Web Analytics to count page views. It sets no cookies and does not follow you across other websites or from one day to the next.

4. Other companies

  • Stripe sets its own cookies on its checkout pages for payment security and fraud prevention. See Stripe's Privacy Policy.
  • Daily Shipped: the badge image in our footer is loaded from dailyshipped.com, which receives your IP address when it loads.
