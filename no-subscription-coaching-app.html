The coaching app you pay for once

Most coaching apps charge every month or every season, and your playbook disappears the day you stop paying. ValenciaSolution Notepads work differently. You pay CA$49.99 once for a coach licence, or CA$499 once for a whole club, and the app is yours to keep.

There's a Notepad for basketball, volleyball, football, baseball, curling, hockey, cricket, soccer, rugby and box lacrosse. Each one runs in the browser on a tablet, phone or computer, with nothing to install.

What you get for one payment

  • A playbook and whiteboard where plays animate, so players see the timing
  • Live game stats or scoring from the bench
  • Roster with player photos and season totals
  • Practice plans and drills (varies by sport)
  • Video recording and review, plus a jersey scanner on six sports
  • Your team data stays in the browser on your own device
