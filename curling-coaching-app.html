What Curling Notepad does

Curling coaching app for club, junior and competitive coaches.

To-scale sheet board

Place stones exactly where they sit in the house and the free guard zone, on a sheet drawn to scale.

Curl paths & takeouts

Draw draws, guards, hits and takeouts with real curl paths. The board counts the end for you.

Animate the shot

Play the shot back so your team sees the line, the weight and what it leaves.

Linescore & hammer

Keep the linescore end by end and always know who has the hammer.

Thinking time

Track thinking time so your team manages the clock in timed games.

Shot percentage

Score every shot and build a shooting percentage for each player over the season.

How curling coaches use it

  1. Before the gameSet up end scenarios on the sheet board: down one without hammer, up two with hammer late.
  2. During the gameKeep the linescore, hammer and thinking time, and score each shot.
  3. After the gameReplay key ends, review shot percentages and watch the video.

Can I try it before I buy?

Yes. The free demo runs in your browser with a sample team already loaded, so you can try every tab before you commit.

Is it free right now?

During the launch offer, the first 1,000 coaches can claim one Notepad free and keep it forever. The offer ends Dec 31, 2026 or at 1,000 sign-ups, whichever comes first.

What devices does it run on?

It runs in the browser on a tablet, phone or computer, so there is nothing to install from an app store. A tablet is the best fit on the sideline.

Where is my team's data stored?

Rosters, player photos, plays, notes and recorded video are stored in the browser on your own device. Only features that sync between coaches, such as live game scoring or team chat, send that data to our hosting providers.
