Volleyball coaching app FAQ

Does it read jersey numbers?

Yes. Point the tablet or phone camera at a player and the Volleyball Notepad reads the jersey number, matches it to your roster and pulls up that player's stats. You can also put names on screen while you record.

Is there a subscription?

No. Volleyball Notepad costs CA$49.99 once for a coach, or CA$499 once for a club licence. There are no monthly or yearly fees.

Can I try it before I buy?

Yes. The free demo runs in your browser with a sample team already loaded, so you can try every tab before you commit.

Is it free right now?

During the launch offer, the first 1,000 coaches can claim one Notepad free and keep it forever. The offer ends Dec 31, 2026 or at 1,000 sign-ups, whichever comes first.

What devices does it run on?

It runs in the browser on a tablet, phone or computer, so there is nothing to install from an app store. A tablet is the best fit on the sideline.

Where is my team's data stored?

Rosters, player photos, plays, notes and recorded video are stored in the browser on your own device. Only features that sync between coaches, such as live game scoring or team chat, send that data to our hosting providers.

Coaching more than one sport?

Every Notepad works the same way, so what you learn in one carries over.
