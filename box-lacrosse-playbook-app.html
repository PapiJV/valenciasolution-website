What Lacrosse Notepad does

Box lacrosse playbook app for minor, junior and club coaches.

Box lacrosse floor board

Diagram plays on a box floor with rounded boards, a centre face-off dot and a crease in front of each net. No field-lacrosse restraining lines or wings.

Animated sets & dodges

Draw cuts, picks and dodges, then animate them so runners see the timing off the ball.

Preset offences

Start from 2-3-1 Motion, 1-4-1 Isolation, Umbrella (power play), X Attack (behind) and the 2-man game (pick and roll).

Preset defences

Man-to-Man Match, Match & Slide Package, Adjacent Slide Package, Penalty Kill Box (4-1) and a full-floor Forecheck Press.

Box-specific roster

Players are tagged by box position: Forward, Transition, Defense and Goaltender.

Drill library & practice plans

Build practices from drills sorted by faceoff, loose ball, dodging, shooting, passing, clearing, transition, special teams and conditioning.

Jersey scanner & video

Record and trim video, then point the camera at a jersey to tag the player on film.

Live game tracking

Track the game as it happens from the bench.

How lacrosse coaches use it

  1. At practicePull drills from the library into a practice plan and walk through tonight's set on the floor board.
  2. Game nightTrack the game from the bench and keep your sets one tap away for timeouts and power plays.
  3. After the gameReview video, trim the key possessions and tag players with the jersey scanner.

Why a box lacrosse board matters

Many lacrosse apps draw a field lacrosse layout, with restraining lines, wings and ten players a side. That isn't the game you coach in a rink in Ontario, B.C. or anywhere box lacrosse is played. Lacrosse Notepad was drawn for the box game from the start: five runners and a goalie on a boarded floor, so your power-play umbrella and penalty-kill box look the way they will on game night.

Lacrosse coaching app FAQ

Does it read jersey numbers?

Yes. Point the tablet or phone camera at a player and the Lacrosse Notepad reads the jersey number, matches it to your roster and pulls up that player's stats. You can also put names on screen while you record.

Does it work for field lacrosse?

The X's & O's board is a box lacrosse floor, and the presets are box sets. Field coaches can still use the roster, practice planner, video and game tracking, but the diagrams are built for the box game.

Is there a subscription?

No. Lacrosse Notepad costs CA$49.99 once for a coach, or CA$499 once for a club licence. There are no monthly or yearly fees.

Can I try it before I buy?

Yes. The free demo runs in your browser with a sample team already loaded, so you can try every tab before you commit.

Is it free right now?

During the launch offer, the first 1,000 coaches can claim one Notepad free and keep it forever. The offer ends Dec 31, 2026 or at 1,000 sign-ups, whichever comes first.

What devices does it run on?

It runs in the browser on a tablet, phone or computer, so there is nothing to install from an app store. A tablet is the best fit on the sideline.

Where is my team's data stored?

Rosters, player photos, plays, notes and recorded video are stored in the browser on your own device. Only features that sync between coaches, such as live game scoring or team chat, send that data to our hosting providers.

Coaching more than one sport?

Every Notepad works the same way, so what you learn in one carries over.
