Refund Policy

Last updated: November 16, 2026

The short version

  • All sales are final. Every Notepad has a free demo, so please try it before you buy.
  • We refund if the app doesn't work as described and we can't fix it, and for duplicate charges.
  • Your rights under Ontario consumer protection law aren't affected.

1. All sales are final

The Notepad apps are digital, delivered the moment you pay, and each has a full free demo you can try first. For those reasons we do not offer refunds or exchanges, except in the cases below.

2. When we do refund

  • If it doesn't work as described. If a Notepad has a defect that stops it working as described on our site, email us within 30 days of purchase with your receipt and a description of the problem. We'll try to fix it. If we can't fix it within a reasonable time, we'll refund you.
  • Duplicate or mistaken charges. If you were charged twice for the same purchase, we'll refund the extra charge.
  • Where the law requires it. Nothing in this policy limits any right to cancel or get a refund that you have under the Ontario Consumer Protection Act or other law that applies to you.

3. How to ask

Email j@valenciasolution.net with the subject "Refund request". Approved refunds go back to your original payment method through Stripe. Your bank decides how long it takes to show up.

4. Free Notepads

The free launch offer involves no payment, so there is nothing to refund.

5. How this fits with our Terms

This page repeats section 7 of our Terms of Sale. If the two ever differ, the Terms of Sale apply.
